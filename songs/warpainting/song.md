# fftechno - song arrangement

Original psychedelic drone rock inspired by The Myrrors' "Warpainting":
**79 BPM, 158 bars = 8:00**, B minor. A slow, dark, hypnotic jam - fuzz guitar
drone, organ, bass, sparse tom-heavy drums, a wah lead and a heavy 6 s reverb.
Synth-only; no samples, no vocals, no video.

Harmony: an 8-bar loop, two bars per chord - Bm | Gmaj7 | Bm | G.

Arc: fuzz drone -> bass/tom -> drums lock in -> organ/tamb -> breakdown ->
wah lead -> peak -> strip back -> outro drone -> fade.
